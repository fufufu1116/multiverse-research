# Casual Post / Brainstorm Intake Doctrine

Status: RESEARCH PROTOTYPE ONLY  
Runtime: OFF

## Purpose

Casual conversation can contain useful opportunity signals, analogies, constraints, growth mechanisms, monetization structures, competitor clues, or falsifiers. The Opportunity Engine should preserve those useful fragments without treating the whole conversation as durable knowledge and without silently upgrading brainstorms into facts.

## Intake rule

The desired flow is:

`casual conversation -> useful fragment extraction -> provenance-bound inbox -> verification/research -> normal Opportunity Engine candidate pipeline`

The inbox is not canonical truth and does not grant adoption or execution authority.

## What should be retained

Retain only material with reusable strategic value, such as:
- a business-model pattern;
- a distribution or growth-loop mechanism;
- a monetization structure;
- a customer pain point or operating constraint;
- a competitor/substitute clue;
- a technology-wave hypothesis;
- a falsifier or reason to kill an idea;
- an analogy that can be converted into a testable strategic question.

Prefer a compact structured extraction over storing entire casual conversations.

## What should not be retained by default

Do not promote or copy into the opportunity inbox:
- ordinary banter with no reusable strategic value;
- sensitive personal information;
- credentials/secrets;
- unverifiable personal/scandal claims;
- deceptive, evasive, manipulative, illegal or unsafe tactics;
- duplicate ideas already represented by a stronger existing candidate;
- numerical/legal/provider-specific claims without marking them unverified.

## Provenance and verification

Every retained item should carry a source identity/time or conversation reference when available, a compact summary, the reusable pattern, and whether concrete claims remain unverified.

A casual-post source is hypothesis evidence only. Before a candidate advances, freshly verify material external claims such as:
- resale/licensing rights;
- provider terms;
- current prices and fees;
- competitor landscape;
- demand and purchase intent;
- legal/compliance constraints;
- expected support burden;
- cancellation/churn assumptions.

## Reseller / value-added resale example

A post proposing purchase of an existing service and resale at a higher monthly price is useful as a pattern, not as validated economics.

Reusable pattern:
`existing service -> lawful resale/agency/OEM right -> owner-specific value add -> self-service subscription -> retained margin`

The apparent arithmetic margin must be replaced by contribution economics:
`sale price - upstream cost - payment fees - tax/compliance cost - refunds/chargebacks - support cost - automation/tooling cost - churn/acquisition burden`

A provider's ordinary end-user subscription must never be assumed resellable. Resale, account sharing, sublicensing, white-label, affiliate, referral and managed-service models are distinct and must be checked against current terms.

## Automation boundary

The repository can define the intake contract and deterministic safety gate. Actual automatic extraction from ChatGPT casual rooms requires an authorized conversation/export/event source capable of supplying those messages. Until such a source exists, do not claim background access to other chats. Manual forwarding of a useful post into this lane remains valid input and should be processed immediately.

Runtime remains OFF. No publication, selling, provider call, spend, account creation, live-business effect, or canonical adoption is authorized by intake.
