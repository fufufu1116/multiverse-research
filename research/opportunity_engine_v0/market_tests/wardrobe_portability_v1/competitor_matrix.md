# Competitor Matrix — Portable Wardrobe Passport v1

Research snapshot: 2026-09-13. This is a desk-research snapshot, not a claim that every product feature is exhaustively known.

| Product/category | Capture / catalog strength | Backup / export evidence reviewed | Resale / recovery overlap | Implication for our wedge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alta | Strong: photo/database/receipt inputs, auto-tagging, low starting threshold | Portable cross-app export was not established from the reviewed official material | Styling/virtual closet focus; no unique whitespace assumed | `fast capture` alone is not a moat |
| Stylebook | Strong: multi-import, web clipping, AI background removal | iCloud backup is documented; broad cross-app portable export was not established from reviewed material | Primarily closet/styling organization | backup exists, so proposition must be portability/migration rather than generic backup |
| Whering | Strong: photo/database/web import | Official FAQ says users cannot locally back up wardrobe data; data is held in Whering database | Closet/styling; white-glove digitization not currently offered | direct evidence that user-controlled local portability can remain unresolved |
| Acloset | Strong smart-closet/category incumbent | Official FAQ says data export requests cannot currently be accommodated | Secondhand-market service was ended; current overlap should not be assumed | direct export-friction evidence |
| Indyx | Strong digital closet + paid human Archivist option | Personal CSV backup available only through support/manual process with a small fee | Strong overlap: styling + resale and resale-listing assistance | broad `closet + resale` is already occupied; migration/control must be sharper |
| Professional cross-listing tools | Listing/import automation optimized for sellers | Varies; data portability is secondary to marketplace operations | Very high professional-reseller overlap | exclude high-volume resellers from initial target |

## Gap interpretation
The evidence does **not** support “nobody offers closet capture, backup, or resale.” Incumbents cover many of those jobs.

The narrower research question is:
> Is cross-app migration + user-controlled portable wardrobe state important enough to drive adoption/payment for ordinary wardrobe owners, especially when paired with under-used-item recovery?

## Competitive invalidation conditions
Revise/kill this wedge if fresh evidence establishes any of the following at broad consumer scale:
1. leading closet apps provide reliable one-click cross-app import/export with full photos/tags/history;
2. users demonstrate negligible switching/data-loss concern despite substantial digitization effort;
3. portability is valued but not enough to trigger adoption or payment;
4. the only strong demand comes from professional resellers already well served by cross-listing tools.

## Evidence discipline
`not established from reviewed sources` means unknown, not absent. Any future update must distinguish official-source facts, community reports, and inference.
