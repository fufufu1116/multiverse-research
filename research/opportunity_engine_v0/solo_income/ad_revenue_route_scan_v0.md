## Purpose
Evaluate advertising income as a solo-owned monetization route, separately from affiliate revenue.

## Route A — Website / calculator media + AdSense later
Current Google official facts:
- AdSense itself has no publisher usage fee.
- For AdSense for Content, publishers receive 80% after the advertiser platform takes its fee; Google gives an example of roughly 68% of advertiser spend when Google Ads is the buy-side platform.
- Google explicitly says actual earnings cannot be predicted precisely because traffic volume, content category, user geography and ad setup materially affect revenue.

Official:
- https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/180195?hl=ja
- https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9902?hl=ja

### Interpretation
AdSense can monetize informational visitors who do not convert to an affiliate program, but it is not a useful first proof when traffic is unproven.

Best fit with current owned-media wedge:
`decision-safe calculator/checker -> organic audience -> affiliate where contextually appropriate -> AdSense as secondary fill`

Do not design the site around ad density before utility is proven.

## Route B — YouTube long-form / Shorts ad revenue
Current YouTube official YPP ad/Premium eligibility for new channels before the announced 2027 change:
- 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 12 months; OR
- 1,000 subscribers + 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days.

Official:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=ja

YouTube also has an expanded YPP entry tier for some fan-funding/shopping features at lower thresholds, but that is not the same as full ad/Premium revenue eligibility.
Official:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=ja

### Announced future threshold change
YouTube currently states that from **2027-02-01**, new creators seeking ads/Premium revenue will need, in addition to 1,000 subscribers:
- 8,000 qualified public watch hours in the prior 365 days; OR
- 20 million qualified Shorts views in the prior 90 days.
Existing YPP members are stated not to lose status solely because of this threshold update.
Official:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009?hl=ja

### Content-quality boundary
YouTube monetization policy clarifies that repetitive/mass-produced content is inauthentic and can be ineligible for monetization; the 2025 policy naming/update emphasized mass-produced content. Therefore a strategy based on bulk low-value AI video generation is not treated as a robust monetization route.
Official:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=ja

## Current strategic comparison
### Website owned-media ads
Pros:
- same research/calculator assets can support affiliate + ads;
- no separate video-production skill or appearance requirement;
- high automation headroom in official-source monitoring and page updates.

Cons:
- revenue density cannot be estimated without traffic;
- search acquisition is competitive;
- ad UX can harm utility if overused.

### YouTube ads
Pros:
- video can build brand/trust and reach beyond search;
- could later repurpose decision-safe findings visually.

Cons:
- meaningful ad-revenue threshold is high and scheduled to become materially higher for new creators on 2027-02-01;
- original/authentic-content requirement makes mass AI production a poor shortcut;
- video editing/voice/visual production increases Owner burden unless automation quality is strong.

## Current disposition
Advertising is **secondary monetization**, not the first cash engine.

Priority:
1. owned calculator/data/search asset with affiliate economics;
2. ads as fill after real traffic exists;
3. YouTube only if content can be repurposed from proven owned-media research without creating a high Owner-hour production burden.

Do not create a channel or AdSense account merely to “reserve the route” under #430 RESEARCH_ONLY.

## Advance criteria
Increase only when there is real audience evidence such as:
- repeat qualified traffic to owned pages;
- enough non-affiliate informational traffic to justify testing ads;
- repeatable video-production workflow with low Owner hours and original value;
- actual audience retention/watch behavior before any ad-income forecast.

## Kill/downrank criteria
- site traffic remains too low for meaningful ad fill after valid testing;
- ads materially reduce calculator usability/trust;
- YouTube requires sustained Owner production burden inconsistent with the solo-automation objective;
- content strategy collapses into mass-produced/reused content risk.
