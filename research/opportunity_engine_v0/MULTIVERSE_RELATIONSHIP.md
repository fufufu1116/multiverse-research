# MULTIVERSE Core vs Opportunity Engine v0 — Research Integration Map

Status: RESEARCH PROTOTYPE ONLY
Runtime: OFF

## Purpose

Clarify the relationship between the canonical MULTIVERSE system and the Opportunity Engine research prototype so the prototype does not accidentally become a second competing operating system.

## Canonical MULTIVERSE role

The canonical MULTIVERSE is the owner-governed, evidence-grounded operating and review system. Its reusable responsibilities include:

- Owner authority and permission gates;
- Core task decomposition, design and integration;
- Vault provenance, artifact identity, recovery and sealed-boundary protection;
- Lab falsification, baseline comparison, failure analysis, calibration, cost and owner-burden attack;
- Auditor evidence sufficiency and promotion/release gates;
- provider-neutral multi-model review/relay;
- canonical state, supersession, rollback/recovery and fail-closed behavior;
- execution authorization boundaries separating research, testing, provider calls, spend, publication and other live effects.

The canonical AI Council loop is broadly:

`Design/Prediction -> Core plan -> Vault verify -> Lab attack -> Auditor gate -> Test/Outcome -> Error analysis -> revision`

MULTIVERSE is therefore the general operating system and constitutional/governance layer. It is not inherently a business-opportunity scanner.

## Opportunity Engine role

Opportunity Engine v0 is a bounded economic-decision capability that may later run inside MULTIVERSE. Its responsibilities are narrower:

- ingest evidence-bearing world signals;
- discover opportunity candidates;
- require competitor, substitute and incumbent/general-AI replacement research;
- model payer clarity, purchase intent, distribution, build cost, demand lifetime and low/base/high economics;
- reason several steps ahead including competitor arrival, commoditization and exit timing;
- distinguish tactical waves, durable human demand and enabling technology waves;
- model diffusion, habituation/immunity, mutation, recurrence, network effects and mutual-value embeddedness;
- search for leverage across distribution, automation, AI, monetization, data, reuse, geography, partners/platforms and infrastructure;
- optimize a bounded combination of leverage options under owner cash/time/risk constraints;
- choose whether to execute internally, sell a bounded signal/playbook, license limited access, use free acquisition, retain internally or reject;
- freeze forecasts before outcomes and learn from actual traffic, conversion, profit, owner time, decay and exit results.

The Opportunity Engine is therefore an economic scout/portfolio/strategy module, not a replacement for MULTIVERSE governance.

## Boundary rule — do not duplicate the core

Opportunity Engine should NOT build independent replacements for:

- Owner permissions and constitutional authority;
- canonical artifact identity / provenance / supersession;
- independent Lab/Auditor gates;
- provider credential and spend authority;
- protected-data / sealed-boundary enforcement;
- cross-provider orchestration infrastructure already supplied by MULTIVERSE;
- recovery and canonical state machinery.

If adopted later, it should consume these as MULTIVERSE services/contracts.

## Proposed future relationship

Conceptually:

`MULTIVERSE OS`

-> governance / authority / evidence / recovery / model orchestration

-> `Opportunity Engine`

-> signals -> candidates -> competition -> economics -> leverage -> strategy -> forecast

-> MULTIVERSE Core/Vault/Lab/Auditor review

-> governed micro-test or HOLD/REJECT

-> measured outcome

-> Opportunity Engine learning history

-> next decision

MULTIVERSE decides what is allowed and whether evidence is sufficient. Opportunity Engine decides what economic opportunity appears attractive and how it may be exploited with the least owner burden and capital risk.

## Why the separation matters

1. The general MULTIVERSE core can serve many domains beyond money-making: research, patents, creative IP, software, science, Keirin or future domains.
2. Opportunity logic can evolve rapidly without weakening canonical governance.
3. A failed business heuristic must not compromise permission, recovery or evidence guarantees.
4. Existing multi-model/provider/review infrastructure can be reused instead of rebuilt.
5. Economic outcome data can become a domain-specific proprietary asset while canonical governance remains domain-neutral.

## Adoption posture

Current canonical vNext state says ordinary new ideas should route to a next-version candidate rather than silently expanding the accepted frozen foundation. Therefore Opportunity Engine remains an isolated Draft research candidate until evidence, tests and an Owner-approved material adoption gate justify integration.

No merge, canonical adoption, Runtime activation, live provider call, credential use, spending, publication, affiliate enrollment or other live economic effect is authorized by this map.
