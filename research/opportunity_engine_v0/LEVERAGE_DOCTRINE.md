# Opportunity Engine v0 — Leverage Doctrine

Status: RESEARCH PROTOTYPE ONLY  
Runtime: OFF

## Fixed principle

Every candidate must be searched not only for whether it is attractive, but for how the same owner time, cash and attention can produce materially more output through leverage.

Leverage discovery is a separate mandatory research pass. Finding one obvious tactic does not mean the candidate is leverage-optimized.

The search should cover at least:
- distribution leverage: existing demand pools, marketplaces, search, social, referrals and other lawful acquisition channels;
- automation leverage: remove repeated owner work with existing automation/services before custom code;
- AI leverage: use current AI to research, generate, classify, personalize, support or operate where it reduces cost/time without turning the product into a thin replaceable wrapper;
- monetization leverage: compare advertising, affiliate/referral, one-time, subscription, transaction fee, bounded output sale/licensing, free acquisition and internal-asset routes;
- data leverage: whether each use/test creates proprietary outcome history or other lawful reusable evidence;
- reuse leverage: whether code, content structure, integrations, audience or workflows can be reused across many opportunities;
- geography leverage: whether the same engine can move across countries/languages where timing, competition or demand differs;
- partner/platform leverage: whether existing ecosystems, APIs, affiliate systems or distribution platforms can provide reach/capability without rebuilding them;
- infrastructure leverage: use commodity hosting, payments, analytics, identity, email/notification and integration layers as replaceable components when practical.

## Do not confuse leverage with scale theater

A claimed multiplier is not enough. Penalize or reject leverage that:
- exposes the core discovery method or makes copying strategically dangerous;
- requires deception, harmful dependence or high legal risk;
- requires a team, sales calls or inventory contrary to the owner's operating constraints;
- adds large recurring fixed cost or vendor lock-in;
- creates owner workload that rises roughly in proportion to revenue;
- depends on an unverified service, feature or market assumption.

The strongest leverage should generally improve one or more of:
- output per owner hour;
- profit per yen at risk;
- distribution per unit of content/product work;
- automation ratio;
- reusable asset creation;
- learning rate from experiments;
- geographic or product reuse;
- reversibility.

## Conservative stacking

Do not multiply every claimed improvement naively. Distribution, conversion, automation and monetization effects can overlap. The v0 estimator gives diminishing credit to additional leverage mechanisms and only counts the strongest few in the first-pass combined estimate.

Real multipliers must eventually be replaced by measured results from the forecast/outcome ledger.

## Relationship to scale path

A tactical candidate may remain tactical even after leverage. The purpose of leverage is not to force every small opportunity into a large business.

Instead ask:
1. can this small opportunity pay back faster or with less owner work;
2. can repeated wins be reused rather than rebuilt;
3. can data/distribution/code accumulated here strengthen the next opportunity;
4. can a verified pattern be promoted into a sustaining automated system;
5. can that system expand without proportional human labor;
6. does selling or exposing the playbook create dangerous competitors;
7. when does the leverage stop working because the market becomes immune, crowded or commoditized.
