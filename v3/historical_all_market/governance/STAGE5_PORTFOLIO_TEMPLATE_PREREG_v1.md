## 3. Conservative car and frame ranking

Car structural score:

`car_consensus_win = min(candidate_a_win_prob, b1a_win_prob)`.

Tie-break: larger arithmetic mean of the two win probabilities, then lower car number.

For frame markets, infer the unique official 7/8/9-car car-to-frame mapping using the already-frozen Stage-1 frame-key inference rule. Frame score is the sum of `car_consensus_win` over active cars in the frame. Tie-break by summed model-mean probability, then lower frame number.

Frame-map ambiguity is FAIL-CLOSED.

## 4. Frozen race-level portfolio templates

Every template operates only on tickets that already pass the selected Stage-3 profile and Stage-4 agreement gate.

### `SINGLE`
Select the one highest-ranked eligible ticket across all sold markets in the race.

### `TOP1_PER_MARKET`
For every sold market, select its highest-ranked eligible ticket. Union across markets.

### `TOP3_PER_MARKET`
For every sold market, select up to its top 3 eligible tickets. Union across markets.

### `TOP5_PER_MARKET`
For every sold market, select up to its top 5 eligible tickets. Union across markets.

### `BOX3`
Choose top 3 consensus-ranked cars for car markets and top 3 consensus-ranked frames for frame markets. Include every consensus-eligible elementary ticket whose ticket participants are entirely inside that selected 3-car / 3-frame set. Published same-frame frame tickets remain valid when their frame key is inside the selected set.

### `WHEEL1x3`
Car markets: axis = top consensus-ranked car; partners = next 3 cars.

- 2shatan: axis fixed first, partner second
- 2shahuku / wide: unordered axis-partner pairs
- 3rentan: axis fixed first; second and third are distinct members of the three-partner set
- 3renhuku: axis plus any two distinct partners

Frame markets: axis = top consensus-ranked frame; partners = next up to 3 frames.

- 2wakutan: axis frame first, partner frame second
- 2wakuhuku: unordered axis-partner frame pair

No implicit same-frame wheel ticket is added unless generated by the explicit rule above; ticket existence is always constrained by the official ticket-key catalog.

### `FORMATION_2x3x4`
Ordered car markets only:

- 3rentan: first position from top 2 cars, second from top 3, third from top 4, all cars distinct
- 2shatan: first from top 2 cars, second from top 3, cars distinct

Ordered frame market:

- 2wakutan: first frame from top 2 frames, second frame from top 3 frames; only officially published ticket keys are admissible, including a same-frame key if it exists and satisfies both positional sets

Unordered markets are not populated by this formation template.
