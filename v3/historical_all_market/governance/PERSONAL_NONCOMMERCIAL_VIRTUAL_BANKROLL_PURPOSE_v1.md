# Multiverse Keirin — Personal / Noncommercial / Virtual-Bankroll Purpose v1

Status: OWNER PURPOSE / GOVERNANCE CONTEXT — NOT A SCIENTIFIC FREEZE
Date: 2026-08-19 JST

## Owner-stated factual purpose

The Owner states that the information collection began as an individual personal hobby and that the research arose afterward.

The project is not intended to:
- place real-money wagers;
- generate real-world betting profit;
- provide betting operations to third parties;
- sell predictions;
- commercialize the collected data;
- redistribute source material as a data service.

All bankroll, stake, return, ROI, drawdown, Kelly and portfolio figures in the research are to be interpreted as **virtual / simulated money only** unless a future governance artifact explicitly changes that status.

## Research-use interpretation

The project objective is scientific/technical evaluation of PRE-only prediction, probability calibration, market comparison and simulated capital management.

Economic metrics remain useful as simulation outputs because they stress-test probability quality and decision rules, but they must not be described as actual wagering performance or realized personal income.

## Data-collection boundary

This factual purpose does not by itself override source terms, access controls, copyright, database rights, privacy rules or technical restrictions.

Therefore the operational boundary remains:
- public/ordinary-access information only;
- no CAPTCHA/WAF/access-control/rate-limit bypass;
- no credential misuse;
- no false identity or false retrospective purpose story;
- minimize copied material where structured factual extraction is sufficient;
- keep source/timestamp provenance for scientific PRE audit;
- no redistribution of collected source material;
- no external provider contact under current Owner policy.

## Scientific consequence

Because real-money operation is out of scope, live BUY/NO-BET and capital-management work is evaluated as a **simulation protocol**.

The research may still require decision-time semantics for odds and PRE information, because those are necessary for avoiding look-ahead bias even in a purely virtual simulation.
