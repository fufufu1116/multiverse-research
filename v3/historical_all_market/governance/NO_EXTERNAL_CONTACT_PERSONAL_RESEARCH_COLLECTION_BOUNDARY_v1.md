# Multiverse Keirin — No External Contact / Personal Research Collection Boundary v1

Status: OWNER POLICY / OPERATIONAL GOVERNANCE — NOT A SCIENTIFIC FREEZE
Date: 2026-08-19 JST

## Owner instruction

External provider contact is prohibited. Do not email, call, submit forms, create vendor accounts, start paid trials, purchase data, negotiate terms, or otherwise contact JKA, Team-Nave, WINTICKET, or another provider unless the Owner later explicitly reverses this instruction.

## Important legal/ethical boundary

The project must not manufacture or rely on a false retrospective story that data was collected for a different purpose. The actual collection purpose and method must be recorded truthfully.

A genuine personal/non-public research workflow is acceptable as an operational objective, but it does not automatically override website terms, copyright/database rights, technical access controls, or other applicable restrictions.

## Allowed research route

The preferred no-contact route is:

1. use already-lawfully-held personal artifacts/data when provenance is known;
2. use publicly viewable official pages for semantic/manual research;
3. where new personal data is collected from public information, preserve only the minimum structured facts necessary for analysis together with source URL, observed-at timestamp, and provenance hash where feasible;
4. keep the resulting research dataset private/non-redistributed unless later legal review supports publication;
5. use the data for internal statistical/information analysis only;
6. do not bypass login, authentication, CAPTCHA, WAF, robots/access restrictions, rate limits, paywalls, or other technical restrictions;
7. do not pretend that public visibility equals permission for bulk automated crawling;
8. if automated collection would conflict with published site policy or technical restrictions, fail closed and fall back to manual/personal acquisition or another already-lawful source.

## Copyright/information-analysis note

Japanese copyright law contains a flexible exception for certain non-enjoyment/information-analysis uses, including AI/data analysis, but this is not an unconditional collection license. The exception may not apply where the use unreasonably prejudices the copyright holder's interests, including conflicts with markets for analysis-ready databases/APIs or circumvention of measures intended to protect such markets.

Therefore this project will not treat `personal hobby` as a blanket legal safe harbor. It will instead rely on truthful purpose, minimal use, no redistribution, no access-control bypass, and source-by-source restrictions.

## Scientific provenance consequence

A personal/manual collection may be scientifically admissible only if it also satisfies PRE-only provenance:
- source identity known;
- observation timestamp known;
- no post-race reconstruction for PRE fields;
- race/line/odds snapshot semantics preserved;
- no future profile values;
- no outcome-derived feature reconstruction.

Legal/operational admissibility and scientific PRE admissibility are separate gates.
