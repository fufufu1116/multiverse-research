## 4. Base ranking model: Plackett–Luce / Harville sequential transform

Let frozen winner probabilities for a race be `p_i`, with `sum_i p_i = 1`.

For distinct cars i, j, k:

`P(i first, j second) = p_i * p_j / (1 - p_i)`

`P(i first, j second, k third) = p_i * [p_j / (1-p_i)] * [p_k / (1-p_i-p_j)]`

No fitted correction coefficient is permitted in v1.

## 5. Car-number market probabilities

### 2車単
For ticket `i-j`:
`P_2shatan(i-j) = P(i first, j second)`.

Market probability sum invariant: 1.

### 2車複
For ticket `i=j` (canonical ascending car numbers):
`P_2shahuku(i=j) = P(i first,j second) + P(j first,i second)`.

Market probability sum invariant: 1.

### 3連単
For ticket `i-j-k`:
`P_3rentan(i-j-k) = P(i first,j second,k third)`.

Market probability sum invariant: 1.

### 3連複
For canonical unordered ticket `a=b=c`:
Sum the six permutations of the exact top-3 order probability.

Market probability sum invariant: 1.

### ワイド
For canonical pair `a=b`:
`P_wide(a=b) = P(a and b both appear somewhere in top 3)`.

Compute by summing exact top-3 order probabilities for every third car and every permutation containing a and b.

Important invariant:
The sum of all Wide ticket probabilities is **3**, not 1, because every realized top-3 set produces exactly three winning Wide pairs.

No Wide odds midpoint, Low, High, or price value is used in Stage 1.

## 10. Promotion boundary

This v1 is a baseline probability transform built from immutable winner probabilities.
It does not assert that Plackett–Luce is economically optimal or perfectly models finish-order dependence.

Any future direct-order model, line-interaction model, fitted correction, or result-trained Stage-1 replacement is a prediction-model semantic change and requires its own governed development lineage before promotion.

Stage 2 may compare frozen ticket probabilities with historical PRICE values only after the Stage-1 artifact passes its outcome-independent quality gate.
Settlement remains prohibited until Stage 1–6 decision rules are frozen.
ECON_HOLDOUT1000 remains SEALED.
